# Physical & Combat Abilities

> [!NOTE]
> In this MUD architecture, physical combat abilities do **not** have a specific level requirement to unlock. Instead, they are universally available at **Rank 1** to any character whose Class grants them the corresponding Skill at character creation (via the `character_classes.starting_skills` schema). Their effectiveness, damage, and success rate scale automatically as your skill rank improves from 1 to 100 through repeated use.

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Backstab | Attempt to backstab an enemy. |
| Bandage | Use a bandage to heal yourself or an ally. |
| Bash | Bash an opponent with your shield. |
| Disarm | Disarm your opponent, knocking their main weapon into their inventory. |
| Hide | Attempt to hide yourself in the shadows. |
| Intimidate | Draw the attention of all enemies currently fighting you. |
| Pickpocket | Attempt to steal an item from someone. |
| Provoke | Draw the attention of all enemies currently fighting you. |
| Taunt | Draw the attention of all enemies currently fighting you. |
| Vanish | Disappear instantly, shedding enemy focus. |
| Warcry | Draw the attention of all enemies currently fighting you. |
